# Professional Cybersecurity Certification Roadmap

A career roadmap mapped by experience tiers and operational specializations.

## Career Progression Tiers

### 1. Foundational / Entry Tier (0–2 Years)
* **CompTIA A+ & Network+**: Hardware, troubleshooting, TCP/IP, and network routing fundamentals.
* **CompTIA Security+**: Universal baseline verifying cryptographic principles, IAM, baseline network defense, and operational security.

### 2. Intermediate / Specialist Tier (2–5 Years)
* **CompTIA CySA+**: Blue team threat detection, behavioral analysis, and incident response operations.
* **CompTIA PenTest+**: Red team hands-on vulnerability assessment, exploit execution, and reporting.

### 3. Senior Technical & Architecture Tier (5–10+ Years)
* **CompTIA SecurityX (CAS-005)**:
  * Replaces legacy CASP+ (CAS-004).
  * 90 questions (PBQs and multiple-choice) | 165 minutes | Pass/Fail.
  * Objective Breakdown: Security Engineering (31%), Security Architecture (27%), Security Operations (22%), Governance, Risk & Compliance (20%).
* **ISC2 CISSP**:
  * Focuses on broad security management, policy architecture, and risk governance across 8 domains.
  * Requires 5 years of verified professional experience in two or more domains.
